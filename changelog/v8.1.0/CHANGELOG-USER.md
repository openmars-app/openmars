## Major update: Earth Withdrawal

Build your economy with Earth support, then keep it running when contact
ends. Choose withdrawal after generation 4, 6 or 8.

- Play with 44 new projects, four preludes and two corporations.
- Receive 2 M€, then 3, 4 and more for consecutive connected generations,
  paid to everyone once all players finish generation setup.
- Import technology to keep Earth-dependent effects working, or use
  cards to change the deadline and temporarily restore contact.
- Check your connected and disconnected production in Game mode, and
  hear dedicated announcements before Earth contact ends.

## Major update: Save and resume

Save your game to a file and continue later on a server with matching
game rules and content.

- Hosts can download a save with Save game in the menu.
- Load game opens a resume lobby where players reclaim their seats.
- Resume pending decisions and keep your game history, including games
  with bots.

## New

- Choose Classic or Earth Withdrawal when creating a game. Classic lets
  you enable Venus and Colonies separately or together.
- Nuke and Nuclear Zone are available in both modes.

## Improved

- Industrial Center has denser factories and pipes. Lava Tube Settlement
  has more surface buildings, vents and exhausts.
- Commercial District has a globe among buildings of varied heights;
  Corporate Stronghold has its own headquarters layout.
- Card symbols and notifications agree, with clearer Earth connection
  requirements and optional payment prompts.
- Alternative card actions appear together as one action with options.
- Game setup is easier to use on phones, with expandable settings.
- The menu and loading screen have new Open Mars artwork.

## Fixed

- Cards received from tile bonuses now appear in the received-cards
  dialog so you can see what you gained.
- Production no longer briefly reappears after choosing cards, and its
  final-generation button says Next instead of Buy cards.
- End-game scores count up before the winner is announced.
- Notifications no longer appear above modal backdrops.
- Card VP tooltips include all scoring conditions.
