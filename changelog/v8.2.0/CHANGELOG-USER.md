## New

- Excentric Sponsor lets you play a card from your hand for 25 M€ less.
- When no legal choice remains for a colony, trade, award or effect, a
  Continue button lets you move on instead of getting stuck.
- A Prelude that cannot be played is discarded for 15 M€.

## Improved

- New icons for card effects, awards, milestones and colonies.
- Card icons show small badges for where a tile goes, what it must be
  next to, which tag it counts and how it scores VP.
- Card amounts appear before their icons, and costs and gains of an
  immediate effect show on one row.
- Card selection shows playability as badges instead of error lists.
- Inactive colonies are labelled and show no markers.
- Choices with only one legal option resolve on their own.

## Fixed

- Oceans you placed no longer count as your own tiles for greenery
  placement.
- Rad-Suits, Sister Planet Support and Urban Decomposers check their
  requirements before you can play them.
- Manutech gains M€ when its M€ production increases.
- Trade is greyed out when you cannot afford the selected payment.
- Free trade only works on your turn.
