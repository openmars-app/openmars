## Major update: A new 3D engine

The board and solar system now run on a new renderer. Your cards, game
rules and saved games stay the same.

- Cities, forests, oceans and special tiles keep their animated worlds.
- The game automatically chooses a supported graphics renderer.
- Visit planets by clicking them or choosing a destination in Travel.
- Shared graphics prepare during loading to reduce first-placement
  pauses, including the first city and Mohole.

## New

- Graphics settings with Low, Medium and High presets, plus individual
  controls for resolution, textures, effects and anti-aliasing.
- FPS limits and a Space brightness slider.
- Saved camera sensitivity, smoother dragging and zooming.
- A Settings window for Controls, Graphics, Audio and AI preferences.
- Performance recordings you can download when reporting a slowdown.

## Improved

- Consecutive tile placements start their animations independently.
- Planet travel keeps the world loaded instead of interrupting play.
- Mobile lobbies have separate Players, Chat, Map and Settings tabs.
- Graphics dropdowns match the game and work with touch or keyboard.
- Sound, music volume and bot-thought visibility are in Settings.

## Fixed

- Reloading no longer briefly shows a black board behind the interface
  before returning to the loading screen.
- Clicking planets changes your view again.
- The Sun no longer has a black circle around its glow.
- Reconnecting restores the correct player or spectator view.
- Renderer errors provide a retry option and clearer details.
